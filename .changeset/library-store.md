Artifacts keeps documents again. Each floor has one library, whose top is its collections, with subcollections, series and subseries below and the org's own names for them. A document is uploaded as it is (up to the manifest's limit) into the app's bucket, and each version moves through its own lifecycle: In progress, In review, Complete, Issued, Retired, every move kept. A version sent for review puts an item in the floor's leads' Inbox, closed once it moves on. Tags (the floor's and the org's, a folder's counting for all inside it), grants to people and teams on the library, a folder or a document, Shared with you, the people picker's search, Find and Home are served too. A deleted document's files are cleared once the retention setting's days pass. The part now asks for its bucket and signs in through the gateway for the Inbox, people and the audit log.
