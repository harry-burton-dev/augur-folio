Its manifest is manifest 3: the rail's places (Home, Library, Shared with you, and From below on team and org floors), its commands and radial actions on documents, folders and collections, five About sections, the retention setting, review in the Inbox, its notification kinds, Find and a Home card. It asks for `inbox:write`, `knowledge:read` and `knowledge:extract` as well as before. The old frontend, the SDK 2 service and the Python rendition worker are gone; contract 3 dropped the old app and its data. The store, reader copies without ML models and the rest of the screens follow.
